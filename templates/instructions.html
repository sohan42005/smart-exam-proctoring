Exam Instructions

Please read the following instructions carefully before proceeding:

  • Camera is required: You must allow webcam access. You must remain visible during the entire exam.
  • Microphone is required: You must allow microphone access. Audio activity is monitored.
  • AI Proctoring: The system continuously monitors for:
    • No face detected
    • Multiple people in the frame
    • Use of mobile phones
    • Suspicious head movements (looking away)

  • Fullscreen: The exam must be taken in Fullscreen mode.
  • Auto-Submit: The exam will automatically submit when the timer ends.
Warning: Any suspicious activity will be recorded along with photo evidence.
